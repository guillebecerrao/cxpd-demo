# Organizational Structure — NexHealth

> Describes the organization where the squad operates: company, product area, team structure, and key roles.
> Read at the start of any onboarding, discovery, or strategy session.

---

## Organization

**Company:** NexHealth — digital health insurer, LATAM operations

**Description:** NexHealth is an insurtech founded in 2019, operating in Chile, Colombia, and Mexico. It offers 100% digital health plans targeting the young adult segment (18–45 years old). It has 500,000 active policyholders and grows at 40% annually. Its core differentiator is the digital experience: enrollment in minutes, paperless care, mobile-first app.

**Area / Division:** Digital Product — Policyholder Experience

**Product:** Coverwise — conversational coverage assistant

---

## Team Structure

### Tribe: Policyholder Experience

Responsible for all digital touchpoints between policyholders and NexHealth.

- **Coverage & Inquiries Squad:** resolves policyholder coverage questions before, during, and after a medical visit. Owner of Coverwise.
- **Onboarding & Policy Squad:** enrollment flow, plan activation, document management.
- **Care & Claims Squad:** provider coordination, reimbursements, emergencies.

### Area: Platform & Data

Infrastructure team that provides coverage APIs and policy data models to the squad.

## Collaboration Model

The squad operates in two-week cycles with lightweight ceremonies. Discovery and delivery run in parallel: while one cycle delivers features, the next is already in exploration. The triad (PM · Designer · Engineer) makes decisions jointly — no approval blockers between roles.

TW acts as an embedded squad: TW consultants work side by side with the NexHealth team, not as external vendors. Knowledge is actively transferred — the goal is for NexHealth to operate autonomously by the end of the engagement.
